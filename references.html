Reference citation

North Carolina State University, Enrollment Management and Services. “Federal Work-Study Program.” Accessed Sept. 9, 2026.

Key facts for reporting

Allowable wage range
$7.25–$15.00 per hour. Higher wages may be approved on a case-by-case basis.
Hours
NC State says students should generally work no more than 20 hours per week in Federal Work-Study positions.
Community service
Off-campus community-service Federal Work-Study positions are listed at $14.00 per hour.
Payment
Work-study earnings are paid directly to students every two weeks rather than automatically applied to the student account.
Article use: Source for the $7.25–$15 wage comparison and 20-hour work-study reference point used in the affordability interactive.

Key facts for reporting

Cost burden
Monthly housing costs, including utilities, exceeding 30% of monthly income.
Severe cost burden
Monthly housing costs, including utilities, exceeding 50% of monthly income.
HUD wording: “Cost burden – Monthly housing costs (including utilities) exceeding 30% of monthly income.”
Article use: Federal benchmark for interpreting rent-burden percentages in the interactive. The 30% threshold is a housing-cost-burden benchmark, not a claim that every individual student should spend exactly 30% of income on rent.
