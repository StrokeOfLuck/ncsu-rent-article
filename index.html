What the HUD 30% rule means: The U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development uses 30% as a housing-cost-burden threshold. Spending 30% or less of income on housing is treated as not cost burdened; spending more than 30% is cost burdened, and more than 50% is severely cost burdened. HUD CHAS definitions HUD compares total household housing costs, including utilities, with household income. This article compares advertised room rent with gross pay and optional aid examples. Loans add borrowed funds, not earnings. The red line provides context; the chart does not classify students as HUD cost burdened.
